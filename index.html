    How it works

    Three steps. No account. No upload.

    1. 01 Drop the PDF Any label from FedEx, UPS, USPS or your store. Multi-page files work too.
    2. 02 Crop automatically Cropr takes the label half of each page and trims the white space. Drag a box to crop it yourself.
    3. 03 Print or save Send it straight to a 4×6 thermal printer or Letter paper, or download a PNG.
    Privacy

    Your labels stay on your computer.

    Cropr runs in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded, and there is no account, no analytics and no cookies. Cropr saves only your theme and paper choices, in your browser's local storage.
